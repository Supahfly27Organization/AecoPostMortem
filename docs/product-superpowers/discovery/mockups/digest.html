The contradiction check is marked inferred on purpose. A first pass using keyword polarity returned 4 candidates and all 4 were spurious — three matched a single bullet against itself, because “do not use it” contains both a prohibition and a recommendation. Contradiction detection needs pairwise comparison with self-matches excluded, and even then two rules about one tool may simply be scoped differently. It will never be an observed check.
